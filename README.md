# neurophysiology-project
University Project Neurophysiology

## Course description
> Many animals devote a large portion of their brain to the neural processing of visual motion information. The fundamental principles of this processing and the properties of so-called motion-sensitive neurons are, in some respects, strikingly similar across species, and even mammals and insects exhibit remarkable similarities in this regard. In this module, the goal is to identify characteristic properties of motion-sensitive neurons in the insect brain by recording their electrical responses to moving stimuli.
